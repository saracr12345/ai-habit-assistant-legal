2. What we collect

Account information

  • Sign in with Apple: a unique Apple account identifier and, if you choose to share them, your name and email address (which may be an Apple private relay address).
  • Email sign-in: your email address and password. Passwords are handled by our authentication provider and stored only in hashed form; we never see them.
  • Guest accounts: an anonymous account identifier, with no name or email.
  • An optional display name, only if Apple provides one when you first sign in.

Habit data

  • Habits you create: title, category, target, schedule and reminder time.
  • Completion records (which habit, which day, when it was marked).
  • Reminders are scheduled on your device as local notifications. We don't run a push-notification server.

Mood check-ins (optional, with your explicit consent)

A daily mood score from 1 to 5, an optional note (up to 500 characters), and whether it was logged by tapping or by voice. Because mood can reveal information about your health, we treat it as special category data under UK GDPR and only store it after you give explicit consent in the app. See Mood consent.

Consent records

When you agree to or withdraw from mood logging, we record when it happened, which version of the consent wording you saw, and (when you withdraw) how many items were deleted. We keep this so we can show that your consent was given and respected. It contains no mood scores or notes.

Voice input

When you speak a habit update, speech is converted to text by Apple's speech recognition service, which may process your audio on Apple's servers under Apple's Privacy Policy. We never receive or store your audio; the app discards it once transcription ends. Only the resulting text is sent to us.

AI feature inputs

  • The text of your spoken or typed habit updates, sent for habit parsing.
  • Messages you send to the AI Coach, with a limited summary of your habits and statistics (for example habit names, streaks and completion rates). Your Coach conversation history is stored only on your device, not on our servers.
  • For Premium reports: statistics your device has already calculated (completion rates, streaks, trends and mood averages, never mood notes). The statistics and the generated report text are saved to your account so the report can be shown again.

Usage counts

A daily count of AI requests per account (habit parsing, Coach messages, reports), used to enforce free-tier limits and prevent abuse.

Premium subscriptions

Purchases are made and paid for through Apple's App Store. We never receive your payment details. To give your account Premium reliably and securely, we verify each purchase with Apple on our server and store a record of your entitlement: the subscription product, whether it was a test (Sandbox) or real purchase, Apple's original and latest transaction identifiers, when your access expires, and whether it was refunded or revoked. We also receive and log Apple's App Store Server Notifications about renewals, expiries and refunds (the notification type, the date, and the original transaction identifier).

So that a purchase can be matched to your AI Habit Assistant account, the app attaches your account's random identifier to the purchase (Apple calls this an app account token). Apple therefore receives that identifier with your purchase. It doesn't include your name or email.

What we don't collect

We don't use advertising, analytics or crash-reporting services, and we don't collect your location, contacts, photos, health-app data or device advertising identifier. We do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Mood consent

  • Giving consent: the app asks before your first mood check-in is saved. Your consent is recorded on our servers, so it applies on all your devices. This step needs an internet connection; everything else in the app, including habit tracking, works offline.
  • Earlier check-ins: if you logged moods before the app asked for consent, we don't include them automatically. You choose whether to include them under your consent or delete them permanently. Until you choose, they stay visible to you but are never used in reports or sent to our AI provider. You can delete them at any time in Profile → Data & Privacy.
  • Withdrawing: you can withdraw at any time in Profile → Data & Privacy → Mood Logging. We then permanently delete all your mood check-ins, and any saved reports that include mood statistics, from your devices and our servers. If you're offline, they're removed from your device straight away and deleted from our servers automatically next time you're online; the app shows this as pending until then. Other devices signed in to your account delete their copies the next time they connect. Withdrawal doesn't affect processing that happened before it.

What you say in habit updates

The full text of what you say or type in a habit update is sent to our AI provider so it can be turned into habits, whether or not mood logging is on. If you mention how you're feeling, that text goes too. Without your mood consent, no mood is suggested or saved from it; with it, the AI may suggest a mood that is only saved if you confirm it. If you'd rather not share how you feel, leave it out of your habit updates.

4. AI features

Habit parsing, the AI Coach and Premium report text are generated by OpenAI (model: GPT-4o mini), which acts as our data processor. Requests go through our own server, never directly from your device, and never include your name, email or password.

  • Our requests tell OpenAI not to store the response for later retrieval.
  • OpenAI doesn't use data sent through its API to train its models. OpenAI may keep request data for up to 30 days, only to detect and prevent abuse, and then deletes it, unless shorter retention applies to our account.
  • The AI only suggests. Nothing it proposes (habits or a mood) is saved until you review and confirm it, and the Coach cannot create, change or delete your data.
  • Streaks, completion rates and other statistics are calculated by the app itself, not by AI.

AI output can be wrong. The app and its Coach give general wellbeing and habit suggestions. They are not medical, psychological or professional advice. If you're worried about your health or mood, please speak to a qualified professional.

AI model development

We are not currently using your personal information to train AI models. We may want to develop our own models in the future. Before doing so we will review the data-protection requirements, explain what information would be used and why, put appropriate safeguards in place, and ask for your separate consent where required. Accepting this policy does not mean you agree to future AI training.

8. Security

  • All data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS).
  • Our database enforces per-account access rules, so each account can only read and change its own data.
  • Secret keys for our AI and database providers are kept only on our servers, never in the app.
  • Our hosting provider encrypts stored data at rest.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your personal data, we will notify you and the ICO where the law requires.

9. Deleting your account

You can permanently delete your account in the app: Profile → Delete Account. This deletes your account, profile, habits, completions, mood check-ins, mood consent records, saved reports, usage counts and Premium entitlement records from our servers. It also erases the app's data for that account on your device, including cached reports and your Coach conversations. If you signed in with Apple, you'll be asked to confirm with Apple, and we revoke the app's access to your Apple ID.

If Apple's service can't be reached, your account and data are still deleted. The app then tells you how to remove AI Habit Assistant from your Apple ID yourself (Settings → your name → Sign in with Apple) and gives you a reference number. We keep only the minimal deletion record described in How long we keep it.

Deleting your account does not cancel an App Store subscription. Manage or cancel it in your Apple ID settings, or through Manage Subscription in the app.

This works the same way for guest accounts. If you can't access the app, email testletlink@gmail.com to ask us to delete your account.

To use these rights, email testletlink@gmail.com. We'll respond within one month. The app doesn't have a self-service export yet, so we'll provide a copy on request.

You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO): ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint, helpline 0303 123 1113. We'd appreciate the chance to resolve your concern first.

11. Children and teenagers

You must be 13 or older to use AI Habit Assistant. We don't knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has used the app, contact us and we'll delete their data.

If you're 13 to 17, please use the app with a parent's or guardian's knowledge. The app is the same for everyone and has high-privacy settings built in: no advertising, no tracking across other apps or websites, no profiling for marketing, no location data, and no social or public features. Mood logging stays off unless you agree to it, and AI suggestions are never saved without your confirmation. The app gives general habit suggestions, not medical or mental-health advice. If you're struggling, please talk to a trusted adult or a professional.
