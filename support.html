Manage or cancel your subscription

Premium is an auto-renewing monthly or yearly subscription, billed by Apple. You can change or cancel it at any time:

  1. In the app: Profile → Manage Subscription, or Profile → Current Plan to see your plan and renewal date.
  2. On your iPhone: Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions → AI Habit Assistant.

Cancel at least 24 hours before your current period ends to avoid the next renewal. Premium stays active until the end of the period you've paid for. If you cancel during the yearly plan's free trial, you won't be charged.

Refunds are handled by Apple. You can request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Restore purchases

If you've subscribed before, for example on another iPhone or after reinstalling the app:

  1. Make sure your iPhone is signed in to the same Apple Account you subscribed with.
  2. Open Profile → Restore Purchases, or tap Restore Purchases on the Premium screen.

If no previous purchase is found, check you're using the right Apple Account, then contact us.

Export your personal data

You can download a copy of your data at any time, even offline:

  1. Go to Profile → Export My Data.
  2. The app prepares a ZIP file and opens the share sheet, so you can save it to Files, AirDrop it or send it.

The export contains CSV files of your habits, completions, habit statistics, reminders, mood check-ins and notes, saved reports, Coach conversations, and profile and preferences, plus your profile photo if you've added one. A README file explains each one.

For CSV files of just your habits, completions and mood check-ins, use Profile → Data & Privacy → Export My Data.

Delete your account

  1. Go to Profile → Delete Account.
  2. Confirm twice. If you signed in with Apple, you'll also be asked to confirm with Apple.

This permanently deletes your account and its data from our servers and from your device, including habits, completions, mood check-ins, reports, Coach conversations and your profile photo. It can't be undone, so export your data first if you want a copy. Guest accounts can be deleted the same way.

Deleting your account doesn't cancel your subscription. Cancel it as described in Manage subscription.

Can't open the app? Email testletlink@gmail.com and we'll delete your account for you. Full details are in the Privacy Policy.

Frequently asked questions

How do I add a habit with my voice?

On the Today tab, tap the microphone bubble and say what you did or want to start, for example "I walked 8,000 steps today and I want to meditate every evening". The app suggests habits for you to review, edit and confirm before anything is saved. You can also add habits by hand with the + button.

What's included for free, and what does Premium add?

Free includes unlimited manual habits, progress statistics, voice habit capture and the AI Coach, with daily limits on voice capture and Coach messages shown in the app. Premium adds daily, weekly and monthly reports, detailed progress insights, personal recommendations and book suggestions. See the Terms of Use for details.

Is there a free trial?

The yearly plan includes a 2-week free trial for eligible new subscribers. The monthly plan has no trial. Apple decides eligibility, and the app shows the offer before you confirm.

I deleted the app. Is my subscription cancelled?

No. Deleting the app or your account doesn't cancel an App Store subscription. Cancel it in Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions.

Can I use the app on more than one device?

Yes. Sign in with Apple or with email and your habits sync to your account. A guest account can only be reopened on the device that created it, so upgrade it to Apple or email sign-in in Profile to keep your data across devices.

Does the app store my voice recordings?

No. Apple's speech recognition turns your speech into text, which may be processed on Apple's servers. We never receive or store your audio; only the resulting text is sent to us to suggest habits.

Does the app work offline?

Habit tracking works offline and syncs when you're back online. Voice habit suggestions, the AI Coach and reports need an internet connection.

Is the AI Coach medical advice?

No. The app gives general habit and wellbeing suggestions, not medical, psychological or professional advice. AI replies can be wrong, so please check them. If you're worried about your health, speak to a qualified professional. In an emergency, call your local emergency number.

How are my streaks calculated?

By the app itself, not by AI. A completed day continues your streak, a day you deliberately mark as skipped doesn't break it, and a scheduled day left not completed resets it.

Do you sell my data or show ads?

No. We don't sell your data, show ads or track you across other apps and websites. Read the Privacy Policy for everything we collect and why.

How do I turn off mood logging?

Go to Profile → Data & Privacy → Mood Logging. Withdrawing consent permanently deletes your mood check-ins and any reports that include mood statistics.
